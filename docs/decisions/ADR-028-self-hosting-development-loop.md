# ADR-028: EngineeringOS self-hosts its own development loop

Status: ACCEPTED
Date: 2026-10-04

## Decision

EngineeringOS is the first managed project of EngineeringOS.

Accepted product discussion must converge into durable intent/decision state, executable work, acceptance evidence, and documentation projections. The system will progressively orchestrate its own development using the same control plane offered to external projects.

## Boundary

Self-improvement does not grant self-authority to change product intent, safety policy, or evidence requirements. Human intent remains authoritative. The system may automate engineering consequences, not silently redefine the desired product.

## Why

A system claiming to hide software engineering from ordinary users must demonstrate that it can absorb its own engineering complexity. Self-hosting is therefore a product acceptance strategy, not a marketing demo.
